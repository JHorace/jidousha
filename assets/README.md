Art the examples load, generated by tools rather than drawn.

Paths are relative to this directory, which is the asset root
(docs/internal/assets.md §2).

- `sprites/hero.png` — 16×16 checkerboard. The squares are asymmetric enough
  that a decode which transposed the image would be caught by
  `examples/load_from_disk.rs`.
- `sprites/glow.png` — 8×8 alpha ramp. The channel a careless RGB→RGBA
  widening loses.
- `sprites/atlas.png` — 32×32, four 16×16 tiles, each a flat colour with a
  darker border and a white notch in its top-left corner. `examples/sprites.rs`
  draws regions of it: the border shows a region that sampled one texel too
  far, and the notch shows a region that arrived flipped or rotated, neither of
  which a flat colour would reveal.
